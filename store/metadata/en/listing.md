## Full description
```
ErbilCafe is a guide to the cafés of Erbil — the ones on 40m and 60m, the
teahouses under the Citadel, the rooftops in Ankawa, and the quiet places to
work in the afternoon.

FIND A CAFÉ
· 253 cafés across the city, on a map or in a list
· Filter by area, price, and what the place offers — Wi-Fi, shisha, outdoor
  seating, a family section, parking
· See what is open right now, at this hour

REAL MENUS, REAL PRICES
Menus with prices in Iraqi dinars, so you know what a cappuccino costs before
you sit down.

BOOK A TABLE
Ask a café for a table and get an answer. A reminder the evening before, so a
booking made on Sunday is not forgotten by Thursday.

SAVE AND SHARE
Keep the cafés you like. Rate a place in one tap, or write what you thought.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
Kurdish, Arabic and English, with full right-to-left layout.

Café information comes from OpenStreetMap, used under the Open Database
Licence, and is corrected by hand.
```
